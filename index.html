FAQ

Answers to common questions.

Everything you need to know before we get started.

How fast can you finish my website? +
Most landing pages take between 3 to 5 days. Full 5–8 page business websites typically take 5 to 7 days. If you have your content and logos ready, I can often deliver an initial working draft within 48 hours.
Will I be able to edit text and photos myself later? +
Yes, 100%. I build using intuitive visual editors (Elementor or clean Gutenberg blocks). You don't need to know a single line of code. I also record a short personalized video walkthrough showing you exactly how to update text, swap photos, or add blog posts.
How do revisions work if I want changes? +
Every project includes unlimited reasonable revisions until you are 100% happy with the outcome. I share a live staging preview link so you can review and suggest adjustments in real time.
What do you need from me to start? +
Just a brief chat! If you have your domain, hosting, logo, and text ready, that is great. If you don't, I will happily recommend fast hosting (e.g., Hostinger, SiteGround) and help you organize your page content from scratch.
Can we work through Fiverr or directly? +
Either way works! If you prefer the buyer protection of Fiverr, we can complete the project on my Level 2 Fiverr profile. If you prefer direct invoicing via PayPal, Wise, or bank transfer, that is also welcome.
